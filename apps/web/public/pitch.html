01 — The problem

Borrowers get liquidated for two reasons. They stack.

Reason one

No warning

Lending markets liquidate the instant a position crosses the line. Most people find out after the penalty is already taken out of their collateral.

Reason two

Can't act in time

Even when you see it coming, adding collateral means moving money from a bank or exchange. That takes hours. Markets move in seconds — so a position you could have saved gets taken apart by a bot.

The result is a brutal, avoidable wealth transfer from ordinary borrowers to liquidation bots.

02 — The solution

Monitor. Warn. Protect.

One layer that closes both gaps — read-only to watch, opt-in to act, never custodial.

Monitor

Read your position

Live Blend positions and pool rates straight from Soroban RPC. No custody, no signing to read.

Warn

Classify the risk

Set an on-chain warning threshold; the Risk Monitor classifies your health factor Safe / Warning / Breached in real time.

Protect

Act before liquidation

Fund a reserve in the Guardian. On breach it releases the reserve to you to defend the position — non-custodial by construction.

Holds an XLM reserve; releases it on breach. Keeper-triggerable, never redirectable.

CCBOH4QO…MGTK

Every mutating call streams typed events; the dashboard reads it all over RPC and explains it. Reads are simulations, writes are signed by the user's own wallet.

04 — Why Stellar

This is only really possible here.

Anchors + SEP

A real fiat bridge

SEP-10/24/6/31 give a regulated path between bank money and on-chain assets, with MoneyGram, Circle and regional anchors already live. "Top up collateral from your card before you get liquidated" is only believable where that bridge exists.

Soroban

Cheap + fast enough to act

~5-second ledgers and sub-cent fees mean we can monitor constantly and take small protective moves without gas making it absurd.

Native USDC + DEX

Route to the cheapest fix

Native USDC, path payments and the built-in DEX let protection swap and route collateral atomically.

Ecosystem

Blend + Reflector

The largest Soroban lending market to build on, and a decentralized oracle for prices.
